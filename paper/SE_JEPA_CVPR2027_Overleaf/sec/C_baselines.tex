\section{Baseline Checkpoints and Comparisons}
\label{app:baselines}

\paragraph{Checkpoint provenance.}
Frozen and AdaJEPA use the official task-specific checkpoints on PushObj and PushT. \ours uses task-trained ResNet world models; its PushObj backbone is trained for three epochs on 16,000 T, L, Z, and plus trajectories. The controlled ablations share this initial checkpoint, so their episodic-reset row isolates memory rather than reproducing the official-checkpoint benchmark. The additional DINO-WM shape model uses the same trajectory count and epoch budget. Its updated PushT CEM checkpoint uses 16,000 newly generated random T-shape trajectories and epoch three. DINO CLS and R3M use T-only checkpoints. Configurations and source records are archived with the paper.

\paragraph{Evaluation groups.}
Frozen, AdaJEPA, and \ours use the paired held-out targets in Appendix~B. External visual baselines use VIS50: 50 initial/goal pairs per condition and seed 100. PushT Shift averages blur, noise, dark, red agent, red object, and red goal; Color averages the last three. The original condition enters the seven-condition curves. Methods within each group share targets and the physical interaction budget.

\paragraph{Published results and deformable references.}
The DreamerV3 and IRIS PushObj Unseen scores are from Zhou et al.~\cite{zhou2024dinowm}: two unseen shapes with both pusher and object targets, compared with our three unseen shapes. Their seven-shape All success and Steps are unavailable and shown as dashes. The cited CDs for DreamerV3, IRIS, R3M, and DINO CLS use ten instances per task. Our DINO-WM and Frozen deformable rows share the same fixed reference on 50 instances. Appendix~B gives the consolidation and unseen-rope protocols.
