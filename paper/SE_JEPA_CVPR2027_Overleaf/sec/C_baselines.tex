\section{Baseline Implementations and Sources}
\label{app:baselines}

\paragraph{Model initialization.}
Frozen and AdaJEPA use the official task-specific checkpoints on PushObj and PushT. \ours uses task-trained ResNet world models; its PushObj backbone is trained for three epochs on 16,000 T, L, Z, and plus trajectories. The additional DINO-WM shape model uses the same trajectory count and epoch budget. Its updated PushT CEM checkpoint uses 16,000 newly generated random T-shape trajectories and epoch three. DreamerV3, IRIS, DINO CLS, and R3M use T-only checkpoints. DreamerV3 and IRIS are frozen offline world-model adapters for continuous-action CEM planning, without reward or policy learning. Configurations and source records are archived with the paper.

\paragraph{Evaluation groups.}
Frozen, AdaJEPA, and \ours use the paired held-out targets in Appendix~B. External visual baselines use VIS50: 50 initial/goal pairs per condition and seed 100. PushT Shift averages blur, noise, dark, red agent, red object, and red goal; Color averages the last three. The original condition enters the seven-condition curves. DreamerV3 and IRIS PushObj results use 50 targets per shape at seed 2000. The Unseen column consistently averages I, square, and small tee for all methods. Methods within each group share targets and the total action budget.

\paragraph{Published results and deformable references.}
The CDs for DreamerV3, IRIS, R3M, and DINO CLS are reported by Zhou et al.~\cite{zhou2024dinowm} on ten instances per task. Our DINO-WM and Frozen deformable rows share the same fixed reference on 50 instances. Appendix~B describes consolidation and the separate evaluation on unseen rope instances.
