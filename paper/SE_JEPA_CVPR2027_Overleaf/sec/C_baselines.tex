\section{Baseline Checkpoints and Comparisons}
\label{app:baselines}

\paragraph{Predictive world models.}
The comparisons focus on latent world models used for visual planning: DINO-WM, LeWM, and DDP-WM, with DINO CLS and R3M as representation baselines. For PushObj and PushT, Frozen and AdaJEPA use the official checkpoints released by AdaJEPA, rather than our trained backbones. For PushObj and PushT, \ours uses ResNet-based world models trained directly on task trajectories. Its PushObj backbone is trained for three epochs on 16,000 trajectories covering T, L, Z, and plus. The additional DINO-WM shape model uses the same training trajectory count and epoch budget, with 4,000 validation trajectories and batch size 32. DINO CLS and R3M use existing T-only checkpoints; LeWM and DDP-WM use their corresponding checkpoint adapters.

The controlled ablation in Table~\mainref{tab:component_ablation} instead starts every variant from our common pretrained checkpoint. Its episodic-reset row isolates memory while holding the backbone fixed; it is separate from the official-checkpoint AdaJEPA benchmark.

\paragraph{Offline generative baselines.}
For PushT we use offline-trained DreamerV3 and IRIS predictors for CEM planning on the same VIS50 targets as DINO-WM. The Unseen column of Table~\mainref{tab:main} includes the published PushObj results of Zhou et al.~\cite{zhou2024dinowm} for DreamerV3 (18\%) and IRIS (14\%, their Table~4). They train on four shapes and test on two unseen shapes, requiring both the pusher and the object to reach their targets. The local results use the seven-shape benchmark in Appendix~B.

\paragraph{Evaluation groups.}
Frozen (pretrained), AdaJEPA, and \ours use the paired held-out targets in Appendix~B. The external visual baselines share the VIS50 targets, with 50 initial/goal pairs in each of six shifted conditions and planning seed 100. Methods within each evaluation group receive the same targets and physical interaction budget. PushT Shift averages blur, noise, dark, red agent, red object, and red goal; Color averages the last three. The original condition contributes to the seven-condition tables and curves.

\paragraph{Deformable results.}
The rope and granular experiments use DINO-WM with pretrained DINOv2 patch features as the frozen backbone. This complements the task-trained ResNet setting used for rigid-object manipulation. AdaJEPA adapts within each episode, whereas \ours uses a consolidated slow predictor. The CD entries for DreamerV3, IRIS, R3M, and DINO CLS come from the ten-instance evaluation of Zhou et al.~\cite{zhou2024dinowm}. Our frozen DINO-WM reference uses the same 50 instances as our deformable adaptation runs and appears in both the DINO-WM and Frozen rows. The repeated-instance and unseen-rope protocols are specified in Appendix~B.
