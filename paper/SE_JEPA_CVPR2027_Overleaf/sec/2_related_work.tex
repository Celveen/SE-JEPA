\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models for control.}
Learned dynamics support control by predicting the consequences of candidate actions. Embed to Control~\cite{watter2015e2c} learns locally linear latent dynamics, World Models~\cite{ha2018worldmodels} learns recurrent simulators, and PETS~\cite{chua2018pets} combines probabilistic ensembles with planning. PlaNet~\cite{hafner2019planet} plans in latent space; Dreamer and its successors learn behavior through latent imagination~\cite{hafner2020dreamer,hafner2021dreamerv2,hafner2025dreamerv3}. SimPLe~\cite{kaiser2020simple} and IRIS~\cite{micheli2023iris} learn visual simulators for data-efficient policy learning. MuZero~\cite{schrittwieser2020muzero} and EfficientZero~\cite{ye2021efficientzero} learn models tailored to search, while TD-MPC and TD-MPC2~\cite{hansen2022tdmpc,hansen2024tdmpc2} combine latent dynamics with value learning for continuous control. We study how a visual planner can accumulate predictive knowledge throughout deployment.

\paragraph{Predictive representations and JEPA.}
Prediction in learned representations is central to LeCun's proposed architecture for autonomous intelligence~\cite{lecun2022path}. CPC~\cite{oord2018cpc} and SPR~\cite{schwarzer2021spr} learn representations through future prediction. DINO and DINOv2~\cite{caron2021dino,oquab2024dinov2} provide transferable visual features, while R3M~\cite{nair2022r3m} learns manipulation features from human video. BYOL~\cite{grill2020byol} predicts target-network embeddings; Barlow Twins~\cite{zbontar2021barlow} and VICReg~\cite{bardes2022vicreg} use redundancy reduction and statistical regularization to learn non-collapsed representations. I-JEPA~\cite{assran2023ijepa} predicts target embeddings from image context, V-JEPA~\cite{bardes2024vjepa} extends feature prediction to video, and V-JEPA~2~\cite{assran2025vjepa2} adds action-conditioned predictions for robotic planning. DINO-WM~\cite{zhou2024dinowm} predicts pretrained patch features and optimizes actions toward goal embeddings. Temporal straightening~\cite{wang2026straightening} improves planning geometry, DDP-WM~\cite{yin2026ddpwm} separates primary dynamics from background updates, and LeWorldModel~\cite{maes2026lewm} learns predictive embeddings end to end from pixels. \ours makes deployment experience persistent within this planning framework, consolidating unfamiliar conditions into predictors that can be retrieved on later encounters. We evaluate this mechanism with gradient-based and sampling-based action optimization.

\paragraph{Test-time adaptation.}
Test-time training~\cite{sun2020ttt} and entropy minimization~\cite{wang2021tent} adapt deployed models using unlabeled target data; continual test-time adaptation follows a changing distribution~\cite{wang2022cotta}. Control supplies a natural self-supervised signal: every executed action yields an observation that checks the prediction. AdaJEPA~\cite{wang2026adajepa} uses this signal inside MPC, with adaptation reset between episodes. \ours builds on this fast loop and retains its experience through slow consolidation, allowing subsequent episodes to start from a predictor informed by earlier interactions.

\paragraph{Continual learning and complementary memory.}
Complementary learning systems pair rapid hippocampal learning with slow neocortical consolidation~\cite{mcclelland1995cls}. Replay revisits earlier experience~\cite{rolnick2019replay}, GEM constrains updates using episodic memory~\cite{lopezpaz2017gem}, and EWC protects important parameters~\cite{kirkpatrick2017ewc}. Learning without Forgetting preserves earlier outputs through distillation~\cite{li2016lwf}; progressive networks retain separate task modules~\cite{rusu2016progressive}. \ours brings complementary memory into predictive control, where retrieving a model changes both the actions taken and the data collected next. Its label-free routing organizes experience into domains, and its readiness gate evaluates consolidated predictors on episodes they have not yet trained on before using them for planning.
