\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models for control.}
World models support predictive planning~\cite{watter2015e2c,chua2018pets,hafner2019planet}, policy learning in learned simulators~\cite{ha2018worldmodels,kaiser2020simple,micheli2023iris,hafner2020dreamer,hafner2021dreamerv2,hafner2025dreamerv3}, tree search~\cite{schrittwieser2020muzero,ye2021efficientzero}, and latent control with value learning~\cite{hansen2022tdmpc,hansen2024tdmpc2}. We focus on goal-image planning, where executed actions provide transition errors for improving the deployed predictor.

\paragraph{Predictive representations and JEPA.}
JEPA and predictive embedding methods learn by predicting representations~\cite{lecun2022path,oord2018cpc,schwarzer2021spr}, alongside view-matching and embedding-regularization objectives~\cite{grill2020byol,zbontar2021barlow,bardes2022vicreg}. Image and video JEPA models extend this principle to action-conditioned planning~\cite{assran2023ijepa,bardes2024vjepa,assran2025vjepa2}, using learned or pretrained visual features~\cite{caron2021dino,oquab2024dinov2,nair2022r3m}. Recent world models study feature learning, latent geometry, and separation of dynamics from backgrounds~\cite{zhou2024dinowm,maes2026lewm,wang2026straightening,yin2026ddpwm}. \ours instead studies how deployment experience can improve the predictor across recurring shifts.

\paragraph{Test-time adaptation.}
Test-time training and entropy minimization update deployed models from unlabeled observations~\cite{sun2020ttt,wang2021tent}, including under changing distributions~\cite{wang2022cotta}. In control, each executed action supplies a target for the dynamics predictor. AdaJEPA~\cite{wang2026adajepa} uses this signal within an episode and resets the weights at its end. JEPA-TTT~\cite{zhang2026jepattt} studies a dynamics shift that persists across episodes. It trains one predictor by replaying overlapping windows from the accumulated interactions. \ours addresses recurring shifts. It keeps the pretrained reference, groups compatible trajectories, and chooses among predictors that have improved on the reference on fresh trajectories.

\paragraph{Continual learning and complementary memory.}
Complementary learning systems pair rapid acquisition with slow consolidation~\cite{mcclelland1995cls}. Continual learners replay earlier experience~\cite{rolnick2019replay}, constrain updates to protect past tasks~\cite{lopezpaz2017gem,kirkpatrick2017ewc}, preserve earlier outputs through distillation~\cite{li2016lwf}, or retain separate task modules~\cite{rusu2016progressive}. For a planner, retrieving a predictor also changes the actions taken and hence the data collected next. \ours uses the first observed transitions of each episode to select among eligible predictors, including the pretrained reference.
