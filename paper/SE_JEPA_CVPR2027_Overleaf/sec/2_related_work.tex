\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{World models for control.}
World models support control in several ways. Embed to Control~\cite{watter2015e2c}, PETS~\cite{chua2018pets}, and PlaNet~\cite{hafner2019planet} predict the consequences of candidate actions for planning. World Models~\cite{ha2018worldmodels}, SimPLe~\cite{kaiser2020simple}, IRIS~\cite{micheli2023iris}, and the Dreamer family~\cite{hafner2020dreamer,hafner2021dreamerv2,hafner2025dreamerv3} use learned simulators to train a policy. MuZero and EfficientZero~\cite{schrittwieser2020muzero,ye2021efficientzero} learn models for tree search, while TD-MPC and TD-MPC2~\cite{hansen2022tdmpc,hansen2024tdmpc2} combine latent dynamics with value learning. We focus on goal-image planning, where a model can learn directly from the discrepancy between a predicted and an observed transition.

\paragraph{Predictive representations and JEPA.}
Predicting future representations is central to JEPA~\cite{lecun2022path} and earlier work on predictive embeddings~\cite{oord2018cpc,schwarzer2021spr}. Self-supervised objectives learn visual features by matching related views or regularizing the embedding distribution~\cite{grill2020byol,zbontar2021barlow,bardes2022vicreg}. I-JEPA~\cite{assran2023ijepa} predicts embeddings of masked image regions, V-JEPA~\cite{bardes2024vjepa} extends this idea to video, and V-JEPA~2~\cite{assran2025vjepa2} conditions predictions on actions for robotic planning. For control, the encoder can draw on pretrained visual or manipulation features~\cite{caron2021dino,oquab2024dinov2,nair2022r3m}. DINO-WM~\cite{zhou2024dinowm} predicts frozen patch embeddings, whereas LeWorldModel (LeWM)~\cite{maes2026lewm} trains its encoder and predictor together from pixels. Temporal straightening~\cite{wang2026straightening} shapes the latent trajectories used for planning, and DDP-WM~\cite{yin2026ddpwm} separates primary dynamics from background updates. Our method addresses how to update the predictor once the agent begins to act.

\paragraph{Test-time adaptation.}
Test-time training and entropy minimization update deployed models from unlabeled observations~\cite{sun2020ttt,wang2021tent}, including under changing distributions~\cite{wang2022cotta}. In control, each executed action supplies a target for the dynamics predictor. AdaJEPA~\cite{wang2026adajepa} uses this signal within an episode and resets the weights at its end. JEPA-TTT~\cite{zhang2026jepattt} studies a dynamics shift that persists across episodes. It trains one predictor by replaying overlapping windows from the accumulated interactions. \ours addresses recurring shifts. It keeps the pretrained reference, groups compatible trajectories, and chooses among predictors that have improved on the reference on fresh trajectories.

\paragraph{Continual learning and complementary memory.}
Complementary learning systems pair rapid acquisition with slow consolidation~\cite{mcclelland1995cls}. Continual learners replay earlier experience~\cite{rolnick2019replay}, constrain updates to protect past tasks~\cite{lopezpaz2017gem,kirkpatrick2017ewc}, preserve earlier outputs through distillation~\cite{li2016lwf}, or retain separate task modules~\cite{rusu2016progressive}. For a planner, retrieving a predictor also changes the actions taken and hence the data collected next. \ours uses the first observed transitions of each episode to select among eligible predictors, including the pretrained reference.
