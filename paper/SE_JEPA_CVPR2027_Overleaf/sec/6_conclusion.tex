\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\ours consolidates informative deployment trajectories into predictors validated for future planning. It improves held-out success with GD and CEM and learns across 14 shape and visual domains with one memory. Replay also reduces deformable goal error, and a rope predictor learned in one pass generalizes to new instances.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Prediction uncertainty and planning outcomes could help the maturity gate determine when a learned predictor is ready for use. Longer observation histories and exploratory actions could distinguish similar dynamics. Better action selection near the goal could turn prediction improvements into successful plans. Longer deployment studies should measure memory growth, retrieval latency, and replay cost alongside planning success. Comparing memory compression with retaining every predictor would help identify practical limits on storage and computation. Tests with rare but recurring conditions would also reveal whether compact memories preserve useful experience or discard it before it is needed again.
