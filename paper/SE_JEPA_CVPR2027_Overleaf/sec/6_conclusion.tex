\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A push taken to reach one goal can help the planner reach the next. \ours records the observed transition, replays it alongside compatible experience, and uses the resulting predictor once it improves on the pretrained reference on new trajectories. On rigid objects, this memory lets the agent solve more held-out goals with fewer replans under both GD and CEM. On deformable objects, replay improves planning with a DINO-WM backbone and transfers to new rope instances after a single pass.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Longer interaction histories could help label-free routing distinguish domains that respond similarly to the first few actions. Late in deployment, lower prediction error does not always translate into more solved goals. Adapting the planning horizon and action search could help the controller put those improvements to use.
