\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\ours reuses deployment experience through selective trajectory storage, replay, and validated retrieval. It improves held-out planning with GD and CEM and learns across 14 recurring shape and appearance domains with one memory. Consolidation also reduces deformable goal error with DINO-WM, and a rope predictor trained in one pass transfers to new instances.

\paragraph{Future work.}
The maturity gate could better balance premature retrieval against delayed reuse by calibrating eligibility to uncertainty and planning outcomes. Longer histories and diagnostic pushes could distinguish similar dynamics, while better near-goal action ranking could turn prediction gains into completed goals. Longer deployment streams would test memory scalability.
